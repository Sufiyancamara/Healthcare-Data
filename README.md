# Healthcare Data Analysis
Analyzing patient retention and behavior involves examining patient engagement, visit patterns, treatment adherence, and overall interactions with healthcare services. This analysis helps identify the factors that influence whether patients continue to seek care, return for follow-up visits, and remain engaged with a healthcare provider over time.

By evaluating trends in patient visits, appointment frequency, cancellations, follow-up rates, and demographic or clinical characteristics, healthcare organizations can better understand patient needs and identify potential barriers to continued care. Understanding these behavioral patterns can also help uncover opportunities to improve patient satisfaction, strengthen patient-provider relationships, and develop targeted strategies to increase retention.

Ultimately, patient retention and behavior analysis provides valuable insights that can support more personalized care, improve the patient experience, and contribute to better long-term healthcare outcomes.
